GitHub Pages hosting

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical usage data such as your IP address, browser and device information, the date and time of requests, referring pages, pages viewed, and links clicked. This processing helps deliver and secure the website.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the reliable and secure public availability of this website. GitHub and its service providers may process data in the countries in which they operate.

Details are available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Email contact

If you contact the provider by email, the address, message, and information you provide are processed to respond to the request. Messages are deleted when the request is complete unless legal retention obligations apply.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own cookies, use analytics or advertising, load external fonts or media, or operate contact forms. It may store your selected light/dark theme in your browser's local storage; this is a local display preference, not tracking. A consent banner is therefore not used. External websites receive data only when you follow a link to them; their own privacy policies then apply.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restriction of processing, data portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
